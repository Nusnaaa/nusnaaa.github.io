About

Engineering through projects.

My background is in aerospace engineering, but much of the work I enjoy sits across the broader mechanical-engineering discipline: designing hardware, understanding how it behaves, testing assumptions and improving a design when the real result differs from the expected one.

My university work has included rocket propulsion, CFD and aeroacoustics, aircraft conceptual design, structural analysis and systems engineering. Alongside that, I am developing personal projects that push me further into mechanical design, electronics, embedded systems and unmanned aircraft.

This portfolio is intended to show more than final renders or finished reports. Where useful, I also document unsuccessful approaches, modelling limitations, design trade-offs and what I would change if I repeated the work.

Current work

Cartography UAS

Personal project · In development

I am currently developing a small unmanned aircraft intended as a long-term practical engineering project.

Rather than starting with a purchased airframe and integrating components into it, I am using the project to work through a more complete engineering process: requirements, conceptual design, preliminary analysis, detailed design, manufacture, integration, ground testing and eventually flight testing.

It is also giving me a reason to develop areas that sat outside the core of my aerospace degree, particularly embedded systems, electronics, software and networked aircraft systems.
