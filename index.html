01 / About

Engineering from concept to analysis and testing.

I am an aerospace engineering graduate with a particular interest in mechanical design, aerodynamics, propulsion, testing and multidisciplinary engineering.

My work has ranged from the design and development of a 500 N hybrid rocket engine to computational investigation of vertical-axis wind turbine aeroacoustics, aircraft conceptual design and the ongoing development of a cartography unmanned aircraft system.

I enjoy projects that require me to move between analytical work and physical engineering: defining requirements, developing concepts, performing calculations and simulations, producing designs, and then understanding how those designs behave when manufactured or tested.
