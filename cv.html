Engineering Projects

VAWT Aeroacoustics →

Individual MEng research project · April–June 2025

Developed a transient model of a three-bladed Darrieus turbine in ANSYS Fluent using Transition SST/SBES; extended it with FW-H acoustics at four receivers and MATLAB analysis. At TSR 3.3 and 9 m/s, predicted Cp ≈ 0.13 versus ≈ 0.27 in the reference study. The discrepancy exposed mesh/time-step and computational limitations; reliable acoustic prediction was not established.

600 N N₂O/HDPE Hybrid Rocket Engine →

Five-person MEng capstone · November 2024–June 2025

Principal responsibility for oxidiser injector design, from concept selection and sizing through CFD, CAD, manufacture and integration, ending in a two-ring impinging configuration. Contributed to shared propulsion sizing and initial nozzle calculations. The 600 N figure was a design target: ignition occurred, but injector–chamber leakage prevented target pressure and sustained combustion.
