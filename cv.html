Assessed University Project

VAWT Aeroacoustics

MEng Aerospace Engineering

Apr-Jun 2025

CFD and aeroacoustic investigation of a three-bladed Darrieus vertical-axis wind turbine using ANSYS Fluent.

  • Reproduced the modelling approach of a published VAWT study using Transition SST combined with SBES.
  • Developed the two-dimensional computational domain, sliding-mesh model, near-wall inflation and local mesh-refinement strategy.
  • Compared aerodynamic performance and flow behaviour against published experimental and CFD reference data.
  • Extended the aerodynamic simulation into an FW-H acoustic analysis using four receivers positioned around the rotor.
  • Investigated the impact of limited computational resources, mesh fidelity, timestep and Courant number on the final aerodynamic and acoustic results.
